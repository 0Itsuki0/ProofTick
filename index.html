Proof Tick

Prove to tick.

Stop checking boxes. Show your work instead.

Proof Tick is a to-do list with one simple rule:
you can't tick a task until you prove you did it.

Add your tasks, then submit a photo when they're done. Your proof becomes part of the completion record.

Whether it's cleaning your desk, taking out the trash, finishing a project, or getting something done around the house, just show it and tick it.
